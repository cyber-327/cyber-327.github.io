Definitions and Formulas

Important Definitions

PLL
Feedback system that makes an oscillator track the phase or frequency of an input.
clock recovery
Extraction of symbol timing from a received signal.
synchronization
Alignment of receiver timing, frequency, or phase with the incoming signal.
capture range
Frequency range over which a PLL can initially acquire lock.
lock range
Frequency range over which an already locked PLL can continue tracking.
receiver
System that selects, amplifies, and recovers information from an incoming signal.
sensitivity
Minimum input level required for a receiver to meet a stated performance target.
selectivity
Ability to accept a desired channel while rejecting nearby signals.
dynamic range
Span between the weakest usable signal and the strongest signal the receiver can handle without unacceptable distortion.
overload
Condition in which a strong input drives a receiver stage outside its useful operating region.
