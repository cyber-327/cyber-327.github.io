Equations and Relationships

  • Wavelength: lambda = propagation speed / frequency
  • SINR = signal power / (interference power + noise power)
  • A 3 dB power change is approximately a factor of two.
Practice focus: Compare wireless observations without decoding payloads or claiming more about identity or activity than the measurements support.
