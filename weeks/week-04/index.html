Recall and Apply

Definitions and Formulas

Important Definitions

capacitance
Ability to store separated electric charge and energy in an electric field; capacitor voltage cannot change instantaneously.
capacitive reactance
Frequency-dependent opposition of a capacitor to AC; it decreases as frequency or capacitance increases.
impedance
Total opposition to AC, combining resistance and net reactance at a stated frequency.
RLC circuit
A circuit containing resistance, inductance, and capacitance.

Equations and Relationships

  • Capacitive reactanceXC = 1 / (2 π f C)
  • Net series reactanceX = XL - XC
  • Series impedance magnitude|Z| = sqrt(R2 + (XL - XC)2)
  • AC current magnitudeIrms = Vrms / |Z|
