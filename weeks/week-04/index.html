Recall and Apply

Definitions and Formulas

Important Definitions

capacitance
Ability to store separated electric charge and energy in an electric field; capacitor voltage cannot change instantaneously.
capacitive reactance
Frequency-dependent opposition of a capacitor to AC; it decreases as frequency or capacitance increases.
impedance
Total opposition to AC, combining resistance and net reactance at a stated frequency.
RLC circuit
A circuit containing resistance, inductance, and capacitance.
resonance
Condition where inductive and capacitive reactances balance; in a series RLC path, impedance is minimized and current peaks.
half-power frequency
Frequency where output power is half its maximum; for like impedances, amplitude is `0.707` of maximum.
quality factor
Ratio of resonant frequency to bandwidth; a measure of response sharpness, not overall design quality.
selectivity
Ability to favor desired frequencies while attenuating others.
bandwidth
Frequency range over which a circuit or channel meets a stated performance criterion.
filter
Circuit or system that changes signal amplitude or phase according to frequency.

Equations and Relationships

  • Capacitive reactanceXC = 1 / (2 π f C)
  • Net series reactanceX = XL - XC
  • Series impedance magnitude|Z| = sqrt(R2 + (XL - XC)2)
  • AC current magnitudeIrms = Vrms / |Z|
  • Series RLC resonancef0 = 1 / (2 π √(L C))
  • Half-power amplitudeAHP = 0.707 Amax
  • BandwidthBW = fH - fL
  • Quality factor relationshipQ = f0 / BW
  • RC cutoff frequencyfc = 1 / (2 π R C)
  • Measured response ratioH = Vout / Vin
  • Interferer relative to desired outputJ / D = Hinterferer / Hdesired
