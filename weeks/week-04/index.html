Equations and Relationships

  • Capacitive reactance: X_C = 1 / (2 pi f C)
  • Series RLC resonance: f_0 = 1 / (2 pi sqrt(L C))
  • Quality factor relationship: Q = f_0 / bandwidth
Practice focus: Predict and then interpret how a resonant or filtered channel separates a desired signal from controlled interference.
