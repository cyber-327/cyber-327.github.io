Definitions and Formulas

Important Definitions

time domain
View of signal amplitude as a function of time.
frequency domain
View of how signal energy or amplitude is distributed across frequency.
fundamental
Lowest-frequency periodic component associated with a repeating waveform.
harmonic
Component at an integer multiple of the fundamental frequency.
spectrum
Representation of a signal's frequency components.
decibel
Logarithmic unit used to express a power ratio or related amplitude ratio.
noise floor
Background level below which weak signals are difficult to distinguish.
SNR
Ratio of desired signal power to noise power.
channel capacity
Theoretical maximum reliable information rate under stated bandwidth and noise conditions.
spectral fingerprint
Repeatable set of measured frequency-domain features used to compare or classify a signal.
resolution bandwidth
Effective frequency width used to separate nearby spectral components or average measured energy.
classification confidence
Stated strength of a classification based on the observed features and remaining alternatives.
