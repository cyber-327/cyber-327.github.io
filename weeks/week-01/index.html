Lecture 01 / Wed

Course Introduction and Electrical Foundations I

September 2, 2026 · 50 minutes

Digital Communications Foundations

  • Course purpose, structure, expectations, and fundamentals-first progression
  • Communication as information represented by a detectable physical change
  • Simple sender-path-receiver model using wired, radio, and optical examples
  • Charge, voltage, current, resistance, power, and the need for a complete circuit
  • Voltage as a difference between two points and current as flow through a path
  • DC levels, changing voltage, and a first definition of an electrical signal
  • Basic source, load, switch, resistor, and ground/reference schematic concepts

Cybersecurity Connection

  • Explain that physical measurements can reveal device state, faults, or modification
  • Compare baseline measurements with new observations before drawing conclusions
  • Set brief legal, ethical, and lab-authorization guardrails

Lecture 02 / Fri

Electrical Foundations II: Ohm's Law and Series Circuits

September 4, 2026 · 50 minutes

Digital Communications Foundations

  • Review voltage, current, resistance, complete paths, and measurement references
  • Engineering notation and common electrical units
  • Ohm's Law and the power formula
  • Series circuits, total resistance, common current, voltage drops, and node voltages
  • Voltage-divider behavior at an introductory level
  • Safe DMM configuration for resistance, voltage, and current
