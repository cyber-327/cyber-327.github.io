Recall and Apply

Definitions and Formulas

Important Definitions

complete circuit
An unbroken conducting path from a source, through a load, and back to the source.
source
A device that supplies electrical energy or a signal to a circuit.
load
A component or circuit that receives power or information from a source.
return path
The path current follows from the load back to the source.
reference
The point against which a voltage is measured, often labeled ground or 0 V.
voltage
Electrical potential difference between two points; measured in volts.
current
Rate of electric charge flow; measured in amperes.
resistance
Opposition to current flow; measured in ohms.
power
Rate at which electrical energy is transferred or converted; measured in watts.
series circuit
A circuit with one current path through all components.
node
A set of directly connected points that share the same voltage.
voltage divider
Series resistors that divide a source voltage in proportion to resistance.
tolerance
The allowed difference between a component's marked and actual value.

Equations and Relationships

  • Ohm's LawV = I R
  • Electrical powerP = V I = I2 R = V2 / R
  • Series resistanceRtotal = R1 + R2 + ...
  • Voltage dividerVout = Vin R2 / (R1 + R2)
Practice focus: Trace a complete current path, identify the voltage reference, calculate series current and voltage drops, and compare the prediction with a measurement.
