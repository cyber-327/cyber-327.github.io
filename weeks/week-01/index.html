Lecture 01 / Wed

Course Framing: Digital Communications as Signal Intelligence

September 2, 2026 · 50 minutes

Digital Communications Foundations

  • Course goals: reliable information transfer over real media
  • Digital communication performance goals: data rate, reliability, cost, range, bandwidth, and security
  • Signal Intelligence framing: observe, classify, measure, reason, and communicate evidence
  • Communication system blocks: source, encoder, transmitter, medium, receiver, decoder, destination, noise

Signal Intelligence Connection

  • Introduce confidentiality, integrity, availability, and availability threats at the channel level
  • Differentiate signal observation from payload access and exploitation
  • Set legal and ethical guardrails for receive-only and lab-owned signal work

Digital Communications Foundations

  • Voltage, current, resistance, power, frequency, and engineering notation

Equations and Relationships
