Recall and Apply

Key Terms and Relationships

Key Terms

  • optical carrier
  • single-mode fiber
  • multimode fiber
  • connector loss
  • bend loss
  • OTDR
  • WDM
  • optical power budget

Equations and Relationships

  • Optical link margin = transmitter power - total loss - receiver sensitivity
  • Total dB loss is the sum of fiber, connector, splice, bend, and design losses.
  • OTDR event distance is based on round-trip propagation time.
Practice focus: Interpret an optical trace or power measurement and compare fiber with copper and wireless under a stated threat model.
