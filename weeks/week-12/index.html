Equations and Relationships

  • Reflection coefficient: Gamma = (Z_L - Z_0) / (Z_L + Z_0)
  • Round-trip distance estimate: d = v t / 2
  • Link loss: loss_dB = input_dBm - output_dBm
Practice focus: Use reflection timing and shape to distinguish cable length, opens, shorts, mismatch, and suspicious inline changes.
