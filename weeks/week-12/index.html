Recall and Apply

Definitions and Formulas

Important Definitions

network medium
Physical path carrying communication signals.
physical layer
Signaling, timing and medium behavior.
data-link layer
Local frame organization, delivery fields and error checks.
single-ended reception
Measuring a signal relative to a reference or return.
differential reception
Measuring the voltage difference between two input conductors.
common-mode pickup
A similar disturbance on both conductors relative to a reference.
crosstalk
Unwanted coupling from a neighboring signal path.
regeneration
Deciding received symbols and retransmitting fresh levels and timing. An incorrect decision can be retransmitted cleanly.
CAN
Controller Area Network, a two-wire differential bus connecting controllers.

Equations and Relationships

  • Differential voltageVdiff = VA - VB
  • Equal pickup in the ideal model(VA + n) - (VB + n) = VA - VB
  • Unequal pickup leaves error(VA + nA) - (VB + nB) = (VA - VB) + (nA - nB)
  • Real noise rejection is finite and frequency dependent; observe receiver input limits.
