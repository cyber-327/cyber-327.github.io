Definitions and Formulas

Important Definitions

carrier
A periodic waveform whose property is varied to carry information.
sideband
Frequency component produced around a carrier by modulation.
AM
Modulation that varies carrier amplitude according to the message.
FM
Modulation that varies instantaneous carrier frequency according to the message.
PM
Modulation that varies carrier phase according to the message.
modulation index
Dimensionless measure of modulation depth or frequency deviation relative to message frequency.
deviation
Maximum departure of instantaneous frequency from the unmodulated carrier.

Equations and Relationships

  • AM bandwidthB = 2 fm,max
  • AM modulation indexm = Vm / Vc
  • FM modulation indexβ = Δf / fm
  • Carson's ruleB ≈ 2(Δf + fm,max)
