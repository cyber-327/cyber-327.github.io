DC-09 service schematic and command reference

1. I/Q state mapping

I is horizontal; Q is vertical. High and low refer to your measured voltage groups. The display positions do not change the physical voltages.

Capture states in time order in Normal mode. The X–Y display shows the possible states, but does not preserve their order. Use CAPTURE for a single 16-symbol message. Read symbols at their midpoints; the lab page gives the Single-acquisition steps.
